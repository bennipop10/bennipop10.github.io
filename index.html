Social Publisher

Social Publisher is a personal, self-hosted tool used by its owner to schedule and publish content to their own social media accounts, including TikTok.

It is not offered as a public service. Only the owner connects their own accounts and publishes their own content.

What it does

  • Connects the owner's social media accounts through each platform's official OAuth login.
  • Uploads and publishes videos created by the owner.
  • Shows basic account information and post statistics to the owner.
