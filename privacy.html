Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Who uses the service

Social Publisher is a personal, self-hosted tool. The only user is its owner, who connects their own social media accounts.

2. Data accessed

When the owner connects a TikTok account, the service receives, with the owner's consent:

  • Basic profile information (display name, username, avatar).
  • Profile details and account statistics (such as follower and video counts).
  • The list of the account's videos.
  • Access tokens required to upload and publish videos on the owner's behalf.

3. How data is used

This data is used only to publish the owner's content and to show the owner their own account information. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

4. Storage

Data and access tokens are stored on the owner's own self-hosted server and are not transferred elsewhere, except to the platform APIs needed to publish content.

5. Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the account in the service or by removing the app's permissions in TikTok's settings. Disconnecting an account deletes its stored tokens.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated at any time. The date above reflects the latest version.
